# Right Point Solutions expanded product catalog

Reference catalog extracted from the public Right Point Solutions product catalog and expanded with five additional products from the same manufacturer families. The archive contains 15 catalog entries.

## Contents
- `catalog.csv` — spreadsheet-friendly catalog with names, categories, descriptions, features, image filenames, and source links.
- `catalog.json` — same catalog as JSON.
- `images/` — verified image files where usable product imagery was available.

## Usage and rights
Descriptions originate from Right Point Solutions and official manufacturer product pages. Images are hosted by Right Point, official manufacturers, or identified source listings, and no reuse license was established for most assets. Before publishing the copy, product images, logos, or trademarks in another shop, obtain permission from Right Point Solutions and the applicable manufacturer/image owner. Public visibility does not automatically grant commercial reuse rights.

No prices, inventory, warranty claims, or unsupported specifications were invented. Confirm current pricing, availability, compatibility, model variants, and image/copy rights before listing.

The Hikvision DS-K1T671 entry has no image file because no verified direct image asset was available from the official page response.

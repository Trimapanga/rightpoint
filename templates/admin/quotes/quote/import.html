What happens

  1. Read. The document is read page by page, including scans and photos.
  2. Match. Items whose SKU or name matches our catalogue are linked, so stock and SKUs follow.
  3. Draft. A new draft quote opens for you to check before you preview, download or send it.

The upload is kept on the quote under Internal for reference and is never printed.
